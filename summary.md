

**Source 1: report.pdf — “Sample Report: Sustainable Urban Travel”**  
This source is a short sample report about sustainable urban travel. It says cities can reduce transport emissions by combining reliable public transport, safe walking and cycling routes, and shared mobility services. It recommends measuring outcomes such as travel time, accessibility, cost, safety, and carbon emissions. It also advises collecting resident feedback, publishing clear indicators, and evaluating pilot projects before expanding them.

**Source 2: https://www.is.fi/matkat/art-2000012245421.html — “Älä toimi näin hotellihuoneessa - Ilta-Sanomat”**  
This source is a Finnish travel article giving advice about hotel rooms. It says guests should not make the bed before leaving because it makes the hotel cleaner’s job harder; sheets are removed anyway, and it is easier to take them off an unmade bed. It also warns against using hotel kettles or coffee makers for things other than drinks, because they can be hard to disinfect. Finally, it advises checking the bed for bedbugs and keeping luggage away from the bed or sofa, ideally on a luggage rack or table.

**Are they related?**  
They are only loosely related in that both concern travel. However, they discuss different topics: Source 1 is about sustainable urban transportation and city planning, while Source 2 is about practical hotel-room etiquette and hygiene.
